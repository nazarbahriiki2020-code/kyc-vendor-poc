# Southeast Asia identity coverage: research template

Global IDV vendors are often weakest in Southeast Asia, where national IDs, digital-ID apps and local data-protection laws vary a lot by country. This template tracks what each vendor **claims** and what was **verified**.

Status legend: ✅ verified in sandbox · 📄 documented by vendor · ❓ not found · ❌ not supported
